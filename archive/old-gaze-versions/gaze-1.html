BlinkBrief gaze tracker

This page uses your webcam to estimate which word you are looking at. Video stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

  • Sit about an arm's length from the screen.
  • Face a light source, not a window behind you.
  • Keep your head fairly still once calibration starts.

Starting the camera

Allow camera access when your browser asks. The first start can take a few seconds.

Calibrate

Look at each dot and click it 5 times. Keep your eyes on the dot while you click.

Accuracy check

Look at the orange dot without moving your mouse.

Read naturally

The word you are looking at gets a teal underline. After 2 seconds on one word it turns amber.
