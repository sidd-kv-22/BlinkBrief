BlinkBrief gaze tracker

This page uses your webcam to estimate which word you are looking at. Video stays in your browser and is never uploaded. Setup takes about two minutes and has three parts: look at nine dots, follow a highlight across the text, then a test. Each part starts with instructions you can read at your own pace, and setup pauses by itself if the camera loses your face.

  • Sit about an arm's length from the screen, camera at eye level.
  • Face a light source, not a window behind you.
  • Keep your head still from now until you finish reading.
  • Keep the browser window the same size, and use a wide window.

Starting the camera

Allow camera access when your browser asks. The first start can take a few seconds.
