Before we start

BlinkBrief is a reading helper. Here is what it does with your reading, in plain words.

  • Your reading stays on this device. BlinkBrief saves which words were hard, how much help you used, and your settings. It never saves audio, video or what you said.
  • Microphone (read-aloud mode). It starts only when you start reading, shows a “Microphone on” label, and stops when you press Pause or Finish. Your browser does the listening: Chrome can do it on this device when it has the language pack, otherwise it may use an online speech service. BlinkBrief tells you which one is in use. Silent mode never uses the microphone.
  • Camera (optional gaze). Off unless you turn it on. The video is worked out inside your browser by WebGazer and is never uploaded. The first time, it needs the internet to load the face-tracking code.
  • Help text. If the optional language-model backend is switched on, only the word and its sentence are sent to it. Never audio, video or your name.
  • You are in control. You can pause, change how eager the help is, or delete everything at any time.
  • If you are a child, please read this with a grown-up. BlinkBrief is not a test and does not diagnose dyslexia.
How would you like to read?

Read at your own pace. Help arrives when a word gets in the way.

BlinkBrief notices when you get stuck, helps in the way that fits, and slowly steps back as reading gets easier.

How it works

  1. ReadRead any text at your own pace. Nothing pops up unless you ask.
  2. NoticeA faint underline appears under a word you may be stuck on.
  3. Get helpHover on it to hear it, see what it means, or make the sentence easier.
  4. GrowAs words get easier, help gets quieter, and you can see how much less you need.

Your privacy

The text you paste or upload stays in your browser. Audio and video are never saved or uploaded. BlinkBrief keeps only which words were hard, how much help you used and your settings, on this device.

BlinkBrief is a prototype reading helper. It does not diagnose dyslexia and does not replace specialist teaching. The cause rules and fading thresholds are hypotheses that have not been tested with learners.
